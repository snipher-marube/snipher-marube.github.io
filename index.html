DawaTrack

Health product traceability

dawatrack.com
Role
Founder and sole engineer
Status
National sandbox checks passed, Nov 2026

Reporting platform that submits medicine traceability events to Kenya's national Track & Trace system for distributors, wholesalers, importers and pharmacy software vendors. GS1 DataMatrix reporting becomes mandatory in Kenya from January 2027.

How it's built

  • One ingestion layer for four sources: SAP Business One, CSV uploads, a partner REST API and warehouse barcode scans. All of them are normalised into a single event model in PostgreSQL, so validation and reporting only have to be written once.
  • Validation runs before anything leaves the system. GS1 identifiers (GTIN, SSCC, GLN) and DataMatrix fields are parsed and checked, and an incomplete event, such as one missing a GLN or expiry date, is held with a specific reason instead of being rejected by the national endpoint.
  • Valid events are serialised to GS1 EPCIS 2.0 and submitted under each business's own national key. Every response from the national system is stored against its event, which gives each client a full audit trail.
  • A separate sandbox environment lets integrators and pharmacy software vendors test their data end to end before going live.

Waks.ai

Voice and SMS access to local opportunities

waks.ai
Role
Lead engineer, team project
Status
Pilot live in Makadara, Nairobi, independently evaluated

Connects young people in Makadara sub-county to catering courses, ward bursaries and local jobs over a plain phone call. No app, data bundle or airtime needed, and callers can speak Kiswahili, Sheng or English.

How it's built

  • Two entry points, one flow. A USSD session or a missed call both hit Africa's Talking webhooks, which trigger a free toll-free callback. The user's session carries through from the menu to the call to the follow-up SMS with their options, fares and referral code.
  • Answers are grounded in a reviewed catalogue. Every option a caller hears or receives must reference a catalogue entry, and a validator blocks any response without one before it is spoken or sent. The service cannot promise a bursary or job that does not exist.
  • The catalogue is built per sub-county from public documents, and each entry is approved by an analyst before it goes live.
  • Privacy is part of the data model. Consent is asked first, questions are limited to what each programme's eligibility rules need, referral codes do not identify the caller, and county officials only see aggregate figures.
  • Callbacks and SMS run as background jobs, so a slow carrier response never holds up a live session.

BWI Digital Skills Academy

Cohort-based learning platform

bwiacademy.com

Online academy where learners work through project-based courses with a cohort. Modules unlock weekly for the whole group.

Learners submit projects, and mentors either approve them or send them back with written feedback. A certificate is issued only once every lesson and assessment is complete. Learners can publish a portfolio of approved work (private by default), and experienced learners can apply to mentor.
