DawaTrack

Health product traceability

dawatrack.com
Role
Founder and sole engineer
Status
National sandbox checks passed, Nov 2026

Reporting platform that submits medicine traceability events to Kenya's national Track & Trace system for distributors, wholesalers, importers and pharmacy software vendors. GS1 DataMatrix reporting becomes mandatory in Kenya from January 2027.

How it's built

  • One ingestion layer for four sources: SAP Business One, CSV uploads, a partner REST API and warehouse barcode scans. All of them are normalised into a single event model in PostgreSQL, so validation and reporting only have to be written once.
  • Validation runs before anything leaves the system. GS1 identifiers (GTIN, SSCC, GLN) and DataMatrix fields are parsed and checked, and an incomplete event, such as one missing a GLN or expiry date, is held with a specific reason instead of being rejected by the national endpoint.
  • Valid events are serialised to GS1 EPCIS 2.0 and submitted under each business's own national key. Every response from the national system is stored against its event, which gives each client a full audit trail.
  • A separate sandbox environment lets integrators and pharmacy software vendors test their data end to end before going live.

Waks.ai

Voice and SMS access to local opportunities

waks.ai
Role
Lead engineer, team project
Status
Pilot live in Makadara, Nairobi, independently evaluated

Connects young people in Makadara sub-county to catering courses, ward bursaries and local jobs over a plain phone call. No app, data bundle or airtime needed, and callers can speak Kiswahili, Sheng or English.

How it's built

  • Two entry points, one flow. A USSD session or a missed call both hit Africa's Talking webhooks, which trigger a free toll-free callback. The user's session carries through from the menu to the call to the follow-up SMS with their options, fares and referral code.
  • Answers are grounded in a reviewed catalogue. Every option a caller hears or receives must reference a catalogue entry, and a validator blocks any response without one before it is spoken or sent. The service cannot promise a bursary or job that does not exist.
  • The catalogue is built per sub-county from public documents, and each entry is approved by an analyst before it goes live.
  • Privacy is part of the data model. Consent is asked first, questions are limited to what each programme's eligibility rules need, referral codes do not identify the caller, and county officials only see aggregate figures.
  • Callbacks and SMS run as background jobs, so a slow carrier response never holds up a live session.

BWI Digital Skills Academy

Cohort-based learning platform

bwiacademy.com
Role
Lead engineer, team project
Status
Platform live, first programmes in preparation

Online academy for Blooming World International where learners work through project-based courses with a cohort, and mentors review real work instead of quiz scores.

How it's built

  • Cohort scheduling drives access. Each cohort has its own start date, and modules unlock for the whole group week by week, so content access is computed from the learner's cohort rather than stored per learner.
  • Project review is modelled as a state machine: submitted, approved, or returned for revision with written mentor feedback. Every transition is recorded, so learners and mentors can see the full history of a submission.
  • Certificates are issued only when every lesson is complete and every assessment has passed. The check runs on the server, so a certificate cannot be generated from a partial record.
  • Role-based access for learners, mentors and admins, including an application flow for experienced learners to become mentors. Learner portfolios are private by default and published only by the learner.

Blooming World International

Non-profit website, East Africa

blwi.org
Role
Backend developer, Blooming World International
Status
Live

Public website for an African-led non-profit working on early childhood development, foundational literacy and numeracy, and youth skills across East Africa.

How it's built

  • Eight languages served from one codebase. URLs are prefixed by locale (/en/, /sw/ and so on), and content models carry a translation per language so editors can publish without touching code.
  • Staff manage projects, research and policy papers, impact reports and news through a custom admin, with reports and publications grouped so they can be filtered by theme and year.
  • A whistleblowing channel takes confidential reports through the site and stores them with access limited to designated staff.

Agrotech Plus

Farm-to-buyer market access, Kenya

agrotekplus.com
Role
Full-stack developer
Status
Live

Platform for an agricultural company that collects produce at the farm gate, grades it and sells it to verified buyers without brokers.

How it's built

  • One account model for farmers and buyers. A user can register as a farmer, a buyer or both, and each role gets its own onboarding form and profile fields.
  • Registrations land in an admin dashboard where the team verifies farmers and buyers before they are matched.
  • Service pages (solar cold storage, irrigation, dryers, field advisories), annual reports, the blog and volunteer sign-ups are all editable by staff, so the site changes without a deploy.
